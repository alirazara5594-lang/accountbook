1. System Introduction & Accounting Foundations

AccountBook is an enterprise-grade ERP designed to unify financial accounting, supply chain, manufacturing, workforce management, and field operations across all business sectors worldwide.

Core Accounting Standards Enforced: All transactional records, journals, inventories, fixed assets, and revenue recognitions strictly follow International Accounting Standards (IAS), International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS), and US GAAP.

1.1 Double-Entry Bookkeeping Principles

The system enforces strict real-time double-entry balancing. Every financial posting requires equal debits and credits:

\(\sum \text{Debits} \equiv \sum \text{Credits}\)  |  Assets = Liabilities + Equity

Out-of-balance entries cannot be posted under any circumstances. If an operational module (e.g. Sales, Purchasing, Payroll) generates a transaction, balanced GL line items are automatically posted in the background.

1.2 Accrual Basis Accounting

Revenues and expenses are recognized when earned or incurred (not when cash is received or paid), fulfilling the Matching Principle (IAS 1).

1.3 Immutable Audit Trail

Posted general journal entries, sales invoices, vendor bills, and tax returns are immutable. They cannot be hard-deleted or altered. Corrections must be made via balanced reversing journals or credit/debit notes with complete user and timestamp tracking.

1.4 Multi-Currency & Real-Time FX Revaluation (IAS 21)

Transactions support foreign currencies (USD, GBP, EUR, AED, SAR, PKR, CAD). Each transaction maintains both the transaction currency value and the functional base currency value. The system computes realized and unrealized foreign exchange (FX) gains and losses upon receipt, payment, and period-end revaluation.

Fixed Assets are recorded at historical cost and depreciated straight-line over useful life. Executing the monthly depreciation run posts:

Debit: Depreciation Expense (64000)

7. Customer Advances & Deferred Revenue (IFRS 15)

Located at Sales & Customers → Deferred Revenue & Advances.

When customers pay annual upfront retainers, software subscriptions, or service deposits before services are rendered, revenue cannot be recognized immediately. It is held as a Current Liability under 23000 Deferred Revenue and amortized monthly into earned revenue.

Operating Procedure:

  1. Click ＋ New Deferred Revenue Contract.
  2. Enter Customer, Contract Title, Total Advance Amount, Start Date, and End Date (e.g. 12 Months).
  3. The system generates a monthly recognition schedule.
  4. Click Recognize Revenue (Batch Run) each month $\rightarrow$ Automatically posts:
Debit: Deferred Revenue (23000) [Liability Decreased]
Credit: Service / Sales Revenue (41000) [P&L Earned Revenue]

8. Procurement & 3-Way Matching Accounts Payable (AP)

To ensure internal control and audit compliance, procurement follows 3-Way Matching:

  • 1. Purchase Order (PO): Specifies authorized quantities and agreed unit pricing.
  • 2. Goods Receipt Note (GRN): Confirms physical warehouse receipt, recording temporary accrued liability:
    Debit: Inventory Asset (13000)
    Credit: GRNI Accrual Clearing (21200)
  • 3. Vendor Bill: Matches against PO and GRN quantities. Upon approval:
    Debit: GRNI Accrual Clearing (21200)
    Debit: Input Tax / Recoverable VAT (14100)
    Credit: Accounts Payable (21100)

9. Vendor Prepayments & Amortization (IAS 1 / GAAP)

Located at Procurement → Prepayments & Amortization and Accounting → Prepayment Schedules.

When paying annual office rent, insurance policies, or software subscriptions in advance, payment is held as a Current Asset under 14000 Prepaid Expenses and expensed monthly.

Operating Procedure:

  1. Click ＋ New Prepayment Schedule.
  2. Enter Title (e.g., Annual Office Rent HQ), Vendor Name, Amount, Start Date, and End Date.
  3. Select Prepaid Expenses (`14000`) and Expense Account (`61000 Rent Expense`).
  4. Click Run Monthly Amortization each month $\rightarrow$ Automatically posts:
Debit: Office Rent / Insurance Expense (61000 / 62000) [P&L]
Credit: Prepaid Expenses (14000) [Asset Decreased]

Bank Reconciliation Engine:

  1. Navigate to Banking & Payments → Bank Reconciliation.
  2. Import bank statement CSV/OFX files under Bank Import.
  3. Click Auto-Match $\rightarrow$ Engine pairs statement lines with GL ledger transactions by Date, Voucher Reference, and Amount.
  4. Review unmatched items to identify uncleared deposits or unpresented checks.

13. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) & Checklists

13.1 Daily Operations SOP Checklist

  • [ ] Enter and post all daily Sales Invoices; email PDF copies to customers.
  • [ ] Record incoming customer payments and apply to invoices.
  • [ ] Receive warehouse goods (GRNs) and match against supplier purchase orders.
  • [ ] Review and approve pending purchase requests and expense claims.

13.2 Month-End Closing SOP Checklist (Step-by-Step)

Order Task Description Module / Menu Location
Step 1 Execute Fixed Asset Depreciation Run Assets & Inventory → Depreciation Run
Step 2 Run Vendor Prepayment Amortization Batch Procurement → Prepayments & Amortization
Step 3 Run Customer Deferred Revenue Recognition Batch Sales & Customers → Deferred Revenue
Step 4 Process, Post, and Disburse Monthly Employee Payrun Payroll & HR → Payroll
Step 5 Import Bank Statements and Complete Reconciliations Banking & Payments → Bank Reconciliation
Step 6 Review VAT / Sales Tax Return & Verify Input/Output Balances Government Compliance → Tax Returns
Step 7 Verify Trial Balance Balances (\(\sum \text{Dr} = \sum \text{Cr}\)) Accounting → Financial Reports → Trial Balance
Step 8 Set Monthly Period Lock Date to prevent backdated edits Accounting → Period Closing

13.3 Year-End Closing SOP Checklist

  • [ ] Conduct comprehensive physical stock cycle count and post adjustments.
  • [ ] Compute IFRS 9 Expected Credit Loss (ECL) allowance on aged receivables.
  • [ ] Execute IAS 21 Multi-Currency Year-End FX Revaluation.
  • [ ] Roll forward Net Income to Retained Earnings (`32000`) and lock fiscal year.
